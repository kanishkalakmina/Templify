# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Templify to be a project where anyone can contribute without being made
uncomfortable. Everyone taking part — maintainers and contributors alike — is expected to
behave with basic professional courtesy.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Critique the code, not the person. "This binding will break on empty arrays" is useful;
  "this is sloppy work" is not.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change. A clear reason is owed to you; agreement is
  not guaranteed.
- Respect that this project is maintained in spare time. Reviews are not instant.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing others' private
information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else the project is
represented.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the maintainer via
[GitHub](https://github.com/kanishkalakmina). Reports are handled discreetly.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block accounts
where behaviour is unacceptable. Repeated or severe violations will lead to a permanent ban.
